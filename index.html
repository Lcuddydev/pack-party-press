About the game

Pack Party is a party game for 4 to 10 players where everyone draws the deck. Draw characters and things (or trace them), and everyone's drawings get shuffled into packs. Rip yours open, and whatever's inside is your hand.

Answer each prompt with a card from your hand, hear every answer read out loud by its player's character, then vote for the best one. Points go to whoever played it, and to whoever drew it.

Every pack hides one of 40+ special cards, like Inflated, Deep Fried or Radioactive, each with its own entrance. Every couple of rounds, choice rounds settle the big questions: would you blow this card up for a million dollars?

Features

  • 4 to 10 players
  • Online: everyone plays on their own copy
  • Public and private online rooms
  • Offline: one copy on the TV, everyone else on their phones
  • 40+ special cards, each with its own entrance
